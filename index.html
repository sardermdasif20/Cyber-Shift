ANALYST DATABASE

Threat Intel

Learn the signals defenders look for. These are educational examples, not live threat feeds.

✉

Phishing

Messages that impersonate trusted people or services to trick someone into sharing information or opening unsafe content.

DEFENSE: Verify the sender through a separate trusted channel. Report suspicious messages.
COMMON
⌁

Unusual login activity

Sign-in attempts that do not fit a user's normal pattern, such as unexpected locations or repeated failures.

DEFENSE: Use multi-factor authentication and review sign-in alerts.
REVIEW
▤

Data exposure

Information becoming accessible to people or systems that should not have access to it.

DEFENSE: Apply least privilege, encryption, and careful access reviews.
REVIEW
◈

Unpatched software

Known weaknesses can remain present when software updates are delayed.

DEFENSE: Keep systems updated and maintain an approved patch schedule.
PREVENTABLE
OPERATOR HANDBOOK

Field Guide

Quick principles for making calm, evidence-based defensive decisions.

01

Pause before you click

Unexpected urgency is a common warning sign. Verify unusual requests independently.

02

Use layered security

Strong unique passwords, a password manager, and multi-factor authentication work together.

03

Keep evidence

Record what happened, when it happened, and which system raised the alert.

04

Escalate responsibly

Follow your organization's incident process. Do not investigate systems without permission.
